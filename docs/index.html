Jobsly (working title)

Your personalized career coach. A short conversation figures out what you're really after, then Jobsly gives you a clear, personal plan: roles worth targeting and your next moves. Apply smarter, not more.

Prototype

PRD v2

Reachable user

Business students in my program who are actively looking for internships or first jobs, and all of them use LinkedIn. Many aren't sure yet which roles fit them, what they pay, or where to start.

Before / after workflow

Before

  • Unsure which roles or industries fit
  • Mass-apply to dozens of jobs and hope something sticks
  • Wait weeks for a career counselor appointment
  • Piece together generic advice from the internet

After (Jobsly)

  • Answer a few quick questions in a coaching chat
  • Get a personal plan: roles to target and three next moves
  • Apply to a few roles that truly fit
  • Come back to your coach anytime

One core workflow

  1. User signs in with a name and email.
  2. Scout, the career coach, asks where they are (student, recent graduate, advancing, or pivoting), their field, goal, work style, skills, and biggest frustration.
  3. The app generates a Career Plan: target roles, three next moves, and roles worth applying to.

Non-goals (for now)

  • No social feed. No posts, likes, or popularity contest. Users keep LinkedIn for that.
  • No mass-apply. No auto-apply bots; Jobsly helps you choose, not spam.
  • No live AI yet. Scout is a scripted, rule-based chat; connecting a language model comes in a later build.

Build log

What works
Sign-in, the full six-step coaching chat (buttons or typed answers), and a Career Plan that changes based on the user's status, field, goal, work style, and skills. Progress is saved in the browser.
What I learned
  • How GitHub repos and GitHub Pages work: the whole product can be hosted free as static files.
  • An API key can't go in a public repo or website. Real AI will need a small server to hold the key.
  • The difference between a rule-based bot, an AI chatbot, and an agent. Scout is currently a rule-based bot.
  • The AI job-search space is crowded, but most tools push mass-applying. Coaching people toward fewer, better-fit applications is an open lane.
What broke or changed
  • Planned live AI onboarding; switched to a scripted chat to keep API keys out of GitHub and ship on time.
  • Pivot: from a general LinkedIn alternative to a personalized career coach. Users keep LinkedIn; Jobsly gives them direction.
  • Renamed from the placeholder "Groundwork" to the working title "Jobsly."
Costs
$0. Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages; no paid services or API calls.
Hours
About 5 hours so far (planning, building, publishing, and updates).
